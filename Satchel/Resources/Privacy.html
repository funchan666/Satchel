Satchel Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information used by Satchel to provide its travel journal, sign-in, subscriptions and optional online content.

Travel records and photos

Trip details, packing lists, notes and photos you create are stored in the app's storage on your device. The app does not currently sync these travel records to its own cloud service. You can edit or delete individual records and export a copy. Removing the app may remove local records. Exported files may contain your personal notes and photos; decide carefully where to save or share them.

Guest sign-in and online service

To request a guest session, the app sends the online service provider a device identifier, your public network address and the app's bundle identifier. It also requests a public network address from an IP lookup service. The app keeps the device identifier in the device Keychain and the session token in memory. The provider may process account, entitlement and online-service information under its own policies. Its server-side retention and deletion practices must be confirmed with that provider.

Camera and photo library

If you choose to take or add a photo, Satchel uses the camera or the system photo picker for the content you select. You can manage camera permissions in your device settings. You can use the journal without adding a photo.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases. Satchel reads verified transaction and subscription status from StoreKit to determine membership access; it does not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy terms.

Advertising

Satchel may show an ad at startup and the connected online service may request interstitial or rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may process device, network and advertising information to provide ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. Google's consent tools may present choices where applicable; an advertising privacy options entry is shown when required. The app uses Google's test advertising configuration during development.

Retention, deletion and choices

Local travel records remain on your device until you delete them, remove app data or uninstall the app. You can delete records within the app and manage device permissions in Settings. A Keychain identifier may persist separately from app storage. For information about deleting online-service data, refer to the service provider's policy and contact the provider.

Children and policy updates

The policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. Review the current policy before using updated features.
