# Satchel action artwork

Created for Satchel with the host-native image tool on 2026-10-29. Three button surfaces use indigo cloth, ivory paper and muted terracotta to distinguish primary, secondary and destructive actions. Individual transparent artwork identifies adding, saving, deleting, selecting a photo, using the camera, sending, filtering and the two favorite states. The design direction is tactile fabric, paper and restrained brass with no embedded labels. Runtime images are in `Satchel/Assets.xcassets`.
