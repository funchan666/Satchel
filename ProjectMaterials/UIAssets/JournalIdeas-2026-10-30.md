# Journal inspiration artwork

Three original transparent illustrations created for Satchel with the host image tool on 2026-10-30. They share the warm paper, navy and natural daylight of the existing journal hero while depicting distinct subjects: a place, a moment, and a photo. These are decorative app illustrations, not user memories or photos. Runtime assets are in `Satchel/Assets.xcassets/JournalIdeaPlace.imageset`, `JournalIdeaMoment.imageset`, and `JournalIdeaPhoto.imageset`.
