# Satchel login visual direction

The login page is a quiet coastal train journey: an open view, warm natural light, and an intimate travel-journal detail. The background is original generated artwork; native text, controls, consent and legal links are rendered separately in SwiftUI. The reference image is a design exploration, not an application screenshot.

Production artwork prompt: Create an original vertical full-bleed background image for the Satchel iOS travel journal app login screen. Cinematic editorial travel photograph, seen from inside an elegant vintage European train: Mediterranean cobalt-blue sea and distant coast through a large window in the upper half, warm late-afternoon light, softly textured linen, an open cream travel notebook and single terracotta ticket at the very bottom edge, natural wood and an olive sprig subtly framing the sides. Rich but controlled blue, butter cream and caramel, calm sophisticated nostalgic travel atmosphere, high-end magazine photography, no people. Reserve a clean softly shaded low-detail area across the lower half for an actual native UI overlay, and a quiet upper-left area for a logo. Portrait 2:3. No text, no lettering, no logos, no icons, no interface, no phone, no device frame, no watermark.
