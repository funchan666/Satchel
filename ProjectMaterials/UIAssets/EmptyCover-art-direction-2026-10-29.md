# Empty cover artwork

Original images created for Satchel's optional trip cover, generated with the host image tool on 2026-10-29.

- `EmptyCover`: a blank photo mat with a blue ribbon, olive sprig and brass clip on warm paper, leaving room for interface text. The empty mat does not represent a user's photo.
- `CoverCamera`: a small cobalt and ivory camera on a transparent background.

Runtime images are in `Satchel/Assets.xcassets/EmptyCover.imageset` and `CoverCamera.imageset`. They are interface artwork and are never saved as trip photos.
