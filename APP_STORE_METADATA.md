## App Name

Satchel Travel Journal

## App Description

Every journey begins with a few things worth remembering. Satchel brings your travel plans, packing lists and memories together in one thoughtful space.

Create a trip with a destination, travel dates and a cover photo. Organize what you need to take with packing lists that include categories, quantities, locations and notes. Check items off as you pack, then use the return list to remember what needs to come home.

Keep a common packing list for the essentials you reach for again and again. Start with suggestions for your trip, reuse a list from an earlier journey and adapt it to the way you travel.

Save the moments that make a place your own. Add dated journal entries, write the stories behind your photos and keep packing photos with notes alongside your trip.

Find your way back to a memory with search, or mark favorite trips to keep them close at hand. Explore practical travel tips for packing, flights, rainy days, budgeting, journaling and the beach.

Your travel journal is stored on your device. Export your trips, lists and photos as a file and share it with the people you choose using apps on your device.

Start with up to three trips, thirty photos and one travel question per day. Satchel Membership gives you more room for trips and photos, along with more travel questions. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be managed in your Apple account.
